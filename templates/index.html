🌱 Crop Disease Detection & Management System

Machine Learning-Based Crop Disease Detection, Pesticide Recommendation and Management

🌿 Single Leaf Diagnosis

Upload an image of a crop leaf. The system will analyze the image, identify the crop disease, provide the confidence level, symptoms, pesticide recommendation and management steps.

📷
Upload a Crop Leaf Image

🌾 Precision Agriculture Field Screening

Upload a wider image containing multiple plants or leaves. The system divides the image into regions and screens each region using the existing crop disease classifier.

🌾
Upload a Wider Crop / Field Image


Note: The underlying classifier was trained and evaluated using individual leaf images from the PlantVillage dataset, not aerial or drone imagery. This module demonstrates a field-level screening approach by applying the existing classifier to regions of a wider image. Actual drone-based deployment would require training and validation using aerial or field-level imagery.
