🌿 Crop Image Analysis

Take or upload a picture of your crop. The system automatically determines how the image should be processed and provides disease detection, pesticide recommendations and management information.

📷
Upload or Take a Crop Picture
You can photograph one leaf, several leaves, several plants, or a wider crop area.
The system will automatically process the uploaded image.

System note: The disease classifier was trained and evaluated using individual crop leaf images from the PlantVillage dataset. For wider crop images, the system automatically divides the image into regions and applies the existing classifier to those regions. This provides a field-screening approach without claiming that the classifier itself was trained on drone or aerial imagery.
