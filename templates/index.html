🌾 Field Scan

Precision Agriculture Field Screening Module

Upload a wider photo showing several plants/leaves. The image is divided into a grid, and each region is screened using the same disease classifier above, to flag which parts of the field may need closer inspection.



Note: The underlying classifier was trained and evaluated on individual leaf images (PlantVillage dataset), not on drone or aerial imagery. This module demonstrates a field-level screening architecture by applying that same classifier to regions of a wider image. Full drone-based deployment would require a model trained and validated on aerial/field-level imagery.
