Precision Agriculture Field Screening

Upload a wider image containing several plants or leaves. The system divides the image into regions and screens each region using the existing EfficientNet disease classifier.

⚠️ Research Note: The underlying classifier was trained and evaluated using individual leaf images from the PlantVillage dataset. This module demonstrates field-level screening by applying the existing classifier to regions of a wider crop image. Drone or aerial deployment would require additional aerial imagery training and validation.
